# Structural Refactor Methodology

Parallel maps that describe one fact can diverge; derive projections from one registry when they share an owner. Keep state facts separate from rendering, and prefer a projection of an existing authority over a mirror with its own precedence rules. Harness-specific phrasing belongs at the harness boundary.

Check behavior at the boundary after structural changes. See [source simplification](source-simplification.md) and [review convergence](review-convergence-gate.md).
