# System overview

Meridian coordinates harnesses rather than controlling their work. The source checkout's `src/meridian/AGENTS.md` and `.context/CONTEXT.md` describe its layers, subsystem ownership and entry points. See [harness abstraction](../concepts/harness-abstraction.md), [state model](../concepts/state-model.md), and [extension system](../concepts/extension-system.md) for the reasons behind those boundaries.
