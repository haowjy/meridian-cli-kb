# Model Resolution Vocabulary

**Alias entry:** a Mars-defined mapping from a human-readable name to model identity and optional routing preferences. A preferred harness does not prove a runnable route. See [aliases and routing](aliases-and-routing.md).

**Fallback candidate:** a concrete profile model-policy entry eligible for prelaunch harness-availability fallback. See [model policies](model-policies.md#fallback-participation).

**Prompting guidance:** caller-facing advice for briefing a model, distinct from its description. See [aliases and routing](aliases-and-routing.md).
